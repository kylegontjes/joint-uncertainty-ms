# Uncertainty in joint reconstruction manuscript

Code and data for the empirical study presented in the joinnt reconstruction uncertainty manuscript. 

Short-read sequencing data for each isolate is found in under the BioProject PRJNA415194. 

For more information about our whole-genome sequencing processing, phylogeny reconstruction, and investigations into the drivers of bβ-lactam/β-lactamase inhibitor resistance in this population, we suggest that you check out the following manuscripts:

1. Kyle J Gontjes, Aryan Singh, Sarah E Sansom, James D Boyko, Stephen A Smith, Ebbing Lautenbach, Evan Snitkin, Phylogenetic Context of Antibiotic Resistance Provides Insights into the Dynamics of Resistance Emergence and Spread, The Journal of Infectious Diseases, Volume 232, Issue 6, 15 December 2025, Pages e992–e1002, https://doi.org/10.1093/infdis/jiaf478

2. Kyle J Gontjes, Ali Pirani, Dhatri Badri Narayanan, Lisa J Lojek, Jennifer H Han, Pam Tolomeo, Ellie JC Goldstein, Ebbing Lautenbach, Evan Snitkin, Identifying drivers of β-lactam/β-lactamase inhibitor resistance emergence and spread before their clinical deployment, medRxiv 2025.07.29.25331838; doi: https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.07.29.25331838
